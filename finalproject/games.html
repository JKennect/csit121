Kingdom Quest

Ranked 'S' by TierDestroyers.Net

A Kingdom in Peril. A Dog From a Mysterious Land.

About Kingdom Quest

Step into a handcrafted pixel adventure filled with ancient ruins, dangerous monsters, mysterious forests, and probably more barrels than necessary. In Kingdom Quest, you'll explore a vibrant fantasy world where every path hides secrets, treasure, and the occasional poor life decision.

Battle strange creatures, uncover forgotten legends, and forge your own journey across kingdoms packed with charm, danger, and suspiciously breakable pottery. Whether you’re hunting for rare loot or simply getting distracted by side quests for three hours, there’s always another adventure waiting beyond the next town.

Built with classic retro inspiration and modern indie spirit, Kingdom Quest is a love letter to the pixel adventures we grew up with, just with significantly more save points. Whether you’re hunting for rare loot, uncovering hidden lore, or chasing rumors of a blue-scarfed traveler, there’s always another adventure waiting beyond the next town.

Shark Survival Crafting

One Raft. Endless Ocean. Too Many Sharks.

About Shark Survival Crafting

Lost at sea with nothing but a tiny raft and questionable survival skills, your only goal is simple: don’t get eaten. In Shark Survival Crafting, you’ll gather drifting resources, expand your floating home, craft tools, and try very hard not to fall into the ocean..

Explore dangerous waters filled with hidden islands, rare materials, mysterious ruins, and sharks that seem way too interested in your raft. Every upgrade brings new opportunities to survive — and new ways for the ocean to ruin your day.

Inspired by classic survival games and packed with retro indie charm, Shark Survival Crafting blends exploration, crafting, and chaotic ocean adventures into one bite-sized survival experience. Because apparently surviving wasn’t difficult enough without sharks involved.

Brave Bark

From Maryland to Myth

Not all Heroes Carry Swords!

About Brave Bark

Before the events of Kingdom Quest, another unlikely hero found themselves lost in the kingdom. Brave Bark follows the adventure of a courageous dog from Maryland who suddenly awakens in a mysterious fantasy world far from home.

Wearing his signature blue neckerchief and armed with little more than determination and a good bark, Brave Bark must platform across dangerous forests, forgotten ruins, towering castles, and hidden caves while searching for answers — and hopefully a way home. Along the journey, he’ll encounter strange creatures, unexpected allies, and the growing darkness surrounding the kingdom itself.

Serving as a prequel to Kingdom Quest, Brave Bark tells the story of how one lost dog would eventually cross paths with the future hero of the kingdom, setting the stage for the adventures still to come.

Inspired by classic retro platformers and modern indie adventures, Brave Bark delivers a heartfelt pixel-art journey filled with exploration, charm, secrets, and plenty of heroic barking.
